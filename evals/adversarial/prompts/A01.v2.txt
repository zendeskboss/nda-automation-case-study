PB01 | Mutuality | severity High
  PREFERRED: Obligations bind both parties equally; Disclosing Party and Receiving Party defined so either party can be either.
  ACCEPTABLE: Obligations stated once and applied to both parties by cross-reference.
  ACCEPTABLE: One-way NDA where ONLY Halyard discloses and the counterparty is the sole recipient.
  DEVIATION: Titled or recited as mutual, but operative obligations or the definition of Confidential Information protect only the counterparty.
  DEVIATION: One-way NDA where Halyard is the recipient and will also disclose.
PB02 | Definition of Confidential Information | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: Information disclosed for the Purpose that is marked confidential OR would reasonably be considered confidential; includes derived notes and analyses.
  ACCEPTABLE: Marked-only definition, where oral or visual disclosures are confirmed in writing within 30 days. (ASSUMED)
  ACCEPTABLE: Standard carve-outs placed inside the definition rather than a separate clause.
  DEVIATION: Definition covers information not disclosed for the Purpose, or information Halyard already held.
  DEVIATION: Definition only covers one party's information (see PB01).
PB03 | Purpose limitation | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: Use solely for discussions regarding a potential commercial relationship between the parties.
  ACCEPTABLE: Purpose limited to a named project, product evaluation or pilot.
  DEVIATION: No purpose limitation, or use permitted 'for any purpose', 'for its business purposes' or similar.
PB04 | Carve-outs | severity High
  PREFERRED: All four: public domain (not via breach), already lawfully held, lawfully received from a third party, independently developed.
  ACCEPTABLE: Different wording or location (including inside the definition) with the same substance.
  ACCEPTABLE: Evidence standard 'written records' or 'reasonable evidence'.
  DEVIATION: Any of the four is missing.
  DEVIATION: Any is narrowed, e.g. independent development only if documented contemporaneously by named personnel, or public domain only if published by the Disclosing Party.
PB05 | Permitted disclosure to Representatives and Group | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: Disclosure to employees, officers, directors, professional advisers, auditors, insurers and Group members on a need-to-know basis; recipient liable for them.
  ACCEPTABLE: Group disclosure only with consent not to be unreasonably withheld, provided advisers, auditors and insurers remain permitted. (ASSUMED)
  ACCEPTABLE: Requirement for written confidentiality undertakings from advisers who are not under professional duties.
  DEVIATION: No disclosure permitted to professional advisers, auditors or insurers.
  DEVIATION: No disclosure to Group members and no consent mechanism.
PB06 | Compelled disclosure | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: Disclosure permitted to the extent required by law, regulator, court or stock exchange; notice and cooperation only where legally permitted; resisting disclosure at the Disclosing Party's cost.
  ACCEPTABLE: Notice 'where lawful' and cooperation at the Disclosing Party's cost.
  ACCEPTABLE: Obligation to use reasonable endeavours to obtain confidential treatment, at the Disclosing Party's cost.
  DEVIATION: No compelled-disclosure exception.
  DEVIATION: Notice required in all cases, with no 'where legally permitted' qualifier.
  DEVIATION: Receiving Party must resist or challenge disclosure at its own cost.
PB07 | Return and destruction | severity Low
  PREFERRED: Return or destroy within 10 Business Days of written request; officer-signed confirmation; exception for automated backups and legally required copies, which stay confidential.
  ACCEPTABLE: Any period of 5 Business Days or more. (ASSUMED)
  ACCEPTABLE: Confirmation by email from an authorised person rather than an officer.
  DEVIATION: No exception for automated backups or copies retained by law or regulation.
  DEVIATION: Period under 5 Business Days.
PB08 | Ownership, licences and IP | severity High
  PREFERRED: All Confidential Information and IP stays with the Disclosing Party; no licence other than use for the Purpose.
  ACCEPTABLE: Statement that each party owns its own IP and no licence is granted (any wording).
  DEVIATION: Any assignment of IP, or ownership of improvements, derivatives, feedback or developments, to the other party.
  DEVIATION: Any licence beyond use for the Purpose, including a licence over feedback that could capture Halyard product information.
PB09 | Warranty and reliance disclaimers | severity Low
  PREFERRED: Information 'as is'; no warranty; no liability for reliance except fraud; no obligation to proceed.
  ACCEPTABLE: Mutual disclaimers in any reasonable wording.
  ACCEPTABLE: Absence of a 'no obligation to proceed' sentence. (ASSUMED)
  DEVIATION: Halyard gives any warranty as to accuracy or completeness of its information.
  DEVIATION: Fraud carve-out removed from a liability exclusion.
PB10 | Publicity | severity Low
  PREFERRED: No announcement or disclosure of the discussions without prior written consent, except as required by law.
  ACCEPTABLE: Mutual publicity restriction in any reasonable wording.
  ACCEPTABLE: No publicity clause at all. (ASSUMED: discussions remain confidential information anyway)
  DEVIATION: Either party may announce the relationship, or use the other's name or logo, without consent.
PB11 | Personal data | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: No personal data beyond business contact details; any other sharing only after a separate data processing or sharing agreement.
  ACCEPTABLE: Counterparty clause committing both parties to UK GDPR / EU GDPR compliance and a separate DPA before sharing personal data.
  DEVIATION: Purpose or clauses indicate personal data (customer, employee or end-user data) will be shared, with no data protection terms or DPA commitment.
PB12 | Term and survival | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: Agreement term 2 years (30 days' notice to terminate early); confidentiality survives 3 years after expiry or termination; Trade Secrets protected while they remain Trade Secrets.
  ACCEPTABLE: Agreement term 1 to 3 years. (ASSUMED)
  ACCEPTABLE: Survival 2 to 5 years. (ASSUMED)
  ACCEPTABLE: Indefinite protection limited to Trade Secrets.
  ACCEPTABLE: No early-termination right.
  DEVIATION: Perpetual or indefinite confidentiality for ALL information.
  DEVIATION: Survival over 5 years, or under 2 years.
  DEVIATION: Termination ends confidentiality obligations immediately.
PB13 | Remedies | severity High
  PREFERRED: Mutual right to seek injunction and equitable relief; remedies cumulative.
  ACCEPTABLE: 'Entitled to seek' rather than 'entitled to'; no requirement to post a bond.
  ACCEPTABLE: Strongly worded but mutual equitable relief.
  ACCEPTABLE: Deletion of the equitable relief clause (relief still available at law). (ASSUMED)
  DEVIATION: Liquidated damages, fixed sums or per-breach payments.
  DEVIATION: Waiver of Halyard's right to seek equitable relief while the counterparty keeps it.
PB14 | Indemnities and liability | severity High
  PREFERRED: No indemnity. Liability for breach left to general law.

PB15 | Non-solicitation | severity Medium
  PREFERRED: No non-solicitation clause.

  DEVIATION: One-way, over 12 months, no general-advertising exception, or covering all staff and contractors.
