MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made the 18th day of November 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) BALLYBRICK SOFTWARE DISTRIBUTION LIMITED, a private company limited by shares incorporated under the laws of Ireland with registered number 584920 and having its registered office at Unit 4, Sandyford Business Centre, Blackthorn Road, Sandyford, Dublin 18, Ireland (hereinafter referred to as "Ballybrick"); and

(2) HALYARD SOFTWARE LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 09876543 and having its registered office at 12 Mill Lane, Manchester M1 2AB (hereinafter referred to as "Halyard").

Ballybrick and Halyard may hereinafter be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Parties are desirous of entering into discussions and evaluating a prospective commercial transaction concerning the potential distribution, reselling and integration of software solutions (the "Permitted Purpose").

(B) In furtherance of the Permitted Purpose, it is contemplated that each Party may disclose to the other Party certain technical, commercial and financial information of a secret and confidential nature.

(C) The Parties hereto have agreed to execute this Agreement to record the terms and conditions governing the protection, disclosure, non-use and preservation of such information.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the subject or context otherwise requires, the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

1.1.1 "Affiliate" means, in respect of any company, any other undertaking that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with, such company from time to time;

1.1.2 "Applicable Law" means any statute, directive, statutory instrument, regulation, order, judgment, decree or rule of law in force from time to time in any relevant jurisdiction;

1.1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or statutory or public holiday in London or Dublin;

1.1.4 "Control" means the beneficial ownership of more than fifty per cent (50%) of the issued share capital or voting rights of an entity, or the legal power to direct or cause the direction of the general management and policies of an entity;

1.1.5 "Disclosing Party" means the Party disclosing or making available Confidential Information, whether directly or indirectly, to the Receiving Party;

1.1.6 "Information" or "Confidential Information" means all information of whatsoever nature and in whatever form or medium (whether oral, written, electronic, pictorial, magnetic, digital or machine-readable) disclosed or made available by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether before, on or after the date hereof, relating to the business, affairs, products, software, source code, object code, system architectures, trade secrets, pricing policies, financial projections, reseller matrices, customer accounts or operations of the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates, which is marked as proprietary or confidential or which by its very nature ought reasonably to be understood by a prudent commercial person to be confidential, together with all analyses, compilations, extracts, notes, memoranda and other records prepared by the Receiving Party containing or reflecting such information;

1.1.7 "Receiving Party" means the Party receiving or obtaining Confidential Information, whether directly or indirectly, from or on behalf of the Disclosing Party; and

1.1.8 "Representatives" means the directors, officers, employees, professional legal advisers, accountants, auditors and corporate consultants of a Party or of its Affiliates.

1.2 In this Agreement:

1.2.1 words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa, and words importing any gender include all genders;

1.2.2 references to persons include individuals, bodies corporate, unincorporated associations, partnerships and any state or state agency;

1.2.3 the headings and sub-headings are inserted for ease of reference only and shall not affect the construction or interpretation of this Agreement; and

1.2.4 references to Clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 In consideration of the mutual disclosure of Confidential Information hereunder, the Receiving Party hereby covenants, warrants and agrees with the Disclosing Party that it shall:

2.1.1 treat, preserve and hold all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in the strictest confidence and not disclose, divulge, impart or publish the same, in whole or in part, to any person or entity whatsoever, save as expressly sanctioned pursuant to this Agreement;

2.1.2 apply to the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party no less a standard of care and degree of security than it applies to its own confidential material of like character, and in any event exercise no less than a high degree of reasonable professional diligence to prevent any unauthorised access, publication, misappropriation or misuse;

2.1.3 utilise and exploit the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party solely and exclusively for the Permitted Purpose, and under no circumstances utilise the same for its own commercial benefit or for any other purpose whatsoever without the antecedent written consent of the Disclosing Party; and

2.1.4 refrain from reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling, probing, translating or otherwise attempting to reconstruct or discover any underlying algorithms, protocols, source code or architectural blueprints of any software, platforms, scripts or technical artifacts furnished by the Disclosing Party.

2.2 The Receiving Party shall forthwith notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon discovering or suspecting any unauthorised use, disclosure, dissemination or loss of any Confidential Information, and shall, at its own expense, co-operate fully with the Disclosing Party in taking all reasonable steps to recover the information and mitigate the effects thereof.

PERMITTED DISCLOSURES

3.1 The Receiving Party shall be entitled to disclose the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party only to such of its Representatives and Affiliates who have a clear and demonstrable need to know the same strictly for the discharge of the Permitted Purpose, provided always that:

3.1.1 the Receiving Party shall, anterior to any such disclosure, fully apprise each such Representative and Affiliate of the strictly confidential character of the Information;

3.1.2 each such Representative and Affiliate is subject to binding confidentiality undertakings or professional obligations of secrecy no less restrictive than those embodied in this Agreement; and

3.1.3 the Receiving Party shall remain primarily liable and fully responsible to the Disclosing Party for any breach of the terms of this Agreement committed by any such Representative or Affiliate as if such breach had been committed by the Receiving Party itself.

EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The covenants, undertakings and restrictions set out in Clause 2 shall not apply to any Information which the Receiving Party can conclusively demonstrate by contemporaneous documentary evidence:

4.1.1 is in or enters the public domain at the time of disclosure, or subsequently becomes generally available to the public otherwise than by default, breach or omission on the part of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

4.1.2 was already lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure hereunder, without any preexisting restriction as to confidentiality or use;

4.1.3 is received on a non-confidential basis from a third party who had the lawful right to disclose such Information without being in breach of any obligation of confidentiality owed directly or indirectly to the Disclosing Party; or

4.1.4 has been independently developed and arrived at by the personnel of the Receiving Party without reference to, reliance upon or derivation from the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party.

MANDATORY AND COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required to disclose any Confidential Information by operation of Applicable Law, valid subpoena, court order or any formal demand of a competent regulatory or supervisory authority, the Receiving Party may disclose such Information strictly to the extent required, provided that, to the extent legally permissible, it shall:

5.1.1 give prompt written notice thereof to the Disclosing Party so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order, injunction or other appropriate remedy;

5.1.2 consult with the Disclosing Party and render all reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's sole expense, in opposing or restricting the scope of such disclosure; and

5.1.3 in the event that disclosure is unavoidable, furnish only that portion of the Confidential Information which is legally mandated, and use all reasonable endeavours to obtain reliable assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to the Information so disclosed.

RETURN AND DESTRUCTION OF MATERIALS

6.1 Subject to Clause 6.2, upon the written demand of the Disclosing Party at any time, or forthwith upon the termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall:

6.1.1 securely deliver up to the Disclosing Party or, at the Disclosing Party's written direction, permanently delete and destroy all documents, media, software, files and materials containing, embodying or derived from the Confidential Information then in its possession, custody or control; and

6.1.2 provide to the Disclosing Party, within fourteen (14) days of such demand, a formal certificate signed by a duly authorised officer confirming full compliance with the provisions of this Clause 6.1.

6.2 Notwithstanding the provisions of Clause 6.1, the Receiving Party shall be entitled to retain one (1) copy of the Confidential Information to the extent strictly necessary for compliance with mandatory legal, regulatory or professional record-keeping obligations, and shall not be obliged to expunge copies created in the ordinary course of business pursuant to routine, automated electronic archival and disaster-recovery backup procedures, provided that any Information so retained shall remain subject to the confidentiality and non-use covenants herein contained for so long as it is retained.

PROPRIETARY RIGHTS AND NEGATIVE WARRANTIES

7.1 All Confidential Information, including all intellectual property rights therein, shall remain the sole, absolute and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party and its licensors. Neither the execution of this Agreement nor the disclosure of any Information hereunder shall be construed as conferring, directly, indirectly or by implication, estoppel or otherwise, any licence, title, patent, copyright, trademark or other proprietary right upon the Receiving Party.

7.2 All Confidential Information is provided by the Disclosing Party on an "as is" basis without warranty of any kind. Neither Party makes any representation, warranty, assurance or covenant, whether express, implied, statutory or otherwise, as to the accuracy, completeness, efficacy, merchantability or fitness for any particular purpose of any Information disclosed.

7.3 Nothing herein shall be deemed to constrain either Party from declining to disclose any specific information, nor shall this Agreement constitute an obligation or commitment on the part of either Party to conclude any further agreement, commercial relationship or business undertaking.

EQUITABLE RELIEF

8.1 The Parties acknowledge and agree that any breach or threatened breach of the obligations contained in this Agreement would cause irreparable harm, damage and injury to the Disclosing Party, for which pecuniary damages alone would not afford an adequate or sufficient remedy.

8.2 In the event of any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled, in addition to any other rights or remedies available to it at law or in equity, to seek and obtain injunctive relief, specific performance and any other form of equitable remedy from any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting any bond, security or other undertaking.

TERM AND DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue in force for an initial term of two (2) years, subject to the right of either Party to terminate this Agreement at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

9.2 Notwithstanding the termination, expiration or cessation of this Agreement howsoever occasioned, the confidentiality and non-use covenants, restrictions and obligations set forth herein shall survive and continue to bind the Receiving Party for a period of three (3) years from the effective date of such termination or expiration, save that in respect of any Information which constitutes a trade secret, such obligations shall endure for so long as the relevant Information retains the character of a trade secret under Applicable Law.

GENERAL AND MISCELLANEOUS

10.2 Neither Party may assign, transfer, charge, sub-contract or delegate any of its rights, benefits or burdens under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, save that either Party may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or to a successor in title pursuant to a bona fide corporate restructuring, merger or sale of substantially all of its assets.

10.3 Any notice, request, consent or communication required or permitted to be given hereunder shall be in writing in the English language and shall be sent by registered post, courier or email to the registered address or designated email address of the relevant Party. Notices delivered by hand or courier shall be deemed served on delivery; notices sent by registered post shall be deemed served three (3) Business Days after posting; and notices sent by email shall be deemed served upon generation of a positive transmission confirmation or on the next Business Day if sent outside standard office hours.

10.4 No waiver by either Party of any breach, default or omission by the other Party shall be construed as a waiver of any continuing or subsequent breach, nor shall any delay or forbearance to exercise any right or remedy operate as an impairment or waiver thereof.

10.5 If any provision of this Agreement is held by any tribunal or court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, void, illegal or unenforceable, such provision shall be severed to the extent of such invalidity, and the remainder of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.

10.6 A person who is not a party to this Agreement shall have no rights whether under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 or otherwise to enforce any term of this Agreement.

10.7 This Agreement contains the entire agreement and understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes and extinguishes all prior agreements, draft agreements, arrangements, representations, warranties and understandings of whatsoever nature between them relating thereto.
