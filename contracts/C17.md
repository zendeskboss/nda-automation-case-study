MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

THIS MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (the Agreement) is entered into as of 14 October 2026 (the Effective Date),

BY AND BETWEEN:

(1) VEDANTA TECHSOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the laws of India with corporate identification number U72200KA2015PTC081234, having its registered office at Cyber Park, Tower B, 4th Floor, Electronic City Phase 1, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560100, Karnataka, India (Vedanta); and

(2) HALYARD SOFTWARE LTD, a company incorporated and registered in England and Wales with company number 09876543, having its registered office at 12 Mill Lane, Manchester M1 2AB, United Kingdom (Halyard).

Vedanta and Halyard may hereinafter be referred to individually as a Party and collectively as the Parties.

RECITALS

(A) The Parties wish to explore and evaluate a prospective commercial and technology collaboration concerning software development, system integration, quality engineering, and IT managed services (the Business Purpose).

(B) During the course of discussions and evaluations relating to the Business Purpose, the Parties intend to share certain proprietary, technical, and commercial information with one another, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, premises, and obligations contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

1.1.1 Affiliate means, with respect to any entity, any other entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such entity, where control means the possession, direct or indirect, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of an entity, whether through the ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

1.1.2 Agreement means this Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, including any schedules, annexures, or amendments hereto executed in writing by authorised signatories of both Parties.

1.1.3 Applicable Law means any statute, law, ordinance, rule, code, regulation, order, decree, or judgment of any governmental, regulatory, or judicial authority having jurisdiction over the relevant Party or subject matter.

1.1.4 Business Day means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or official public holiday on which commercial banks are open for regular business in Bengaluru, India, or London, England.

1.1.5 Confidential Information means any and all proprietary, non-public, or sensitive information, whether technical, business, financial, operational, or legal, in whatever form recorded or communicated (including oral, visual, written, electronic, magnetic, or digital formats), disclosed by or on behalf of Vedanta to Halyard, or otherwise obtained or accessed by Halyard in connection with the Business Purpose, whether disclosed prior to, on, or after the Effective Date. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
1.1.5.1 all software, computer programs, source code, object code, APIs, technical architectures, database schemas, scripts, algorithms, data models, formats, interfaces, and development tools;
1.1.5.2 all operational data, customer lists, vendor details, pricing models, margin sheets, cost breakdowns, financial forecasts, billing records, strategic plans, and product roadmaps;
1.1.5.3 all employee profiles, contractor records, personal identification data, performance appraisals, compensation details, and candidate CVs supplied in connection with prospective resource allocation or project staffing;
1.1.5.4 all research notes, inventions, ideas, discoveries, processes, know-how, designs, drawings, trade secrets, techniques, and specifications; and
1.1.5.5 all analyses, compilations, extracts, summaries, notes, working papers, or derivative works prepared by Halyard or its Representatives that contain, reflect, or are generated from any such information.

1.1.6 Disclosing Party means the Party disclosing Confidential Information under this Agreement.

1.1.7 Receiving Party means the Party receiving Confidential Information under this Agreement.

1.1.8 Representatives means the directors, officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants, and consultants of a Party and of its Affiliates who have a legitimate need to access Confidential Information for the Business Purpose.

SCOPE AND NATURE OF CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 Halyard acknowledges and agrees that the Confidential Information disclosed to it contains valuable business assets and trade secrets of Vedanta. Accordingly, Halyard undertakes to:
2.1.1 hold and maintain the Confidential Information in strict confidence, exercising at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable standard of care;
2.1.2 restrict the disclosure of Confidential Information solely to those of its Representatives who strictly need to know such information for the furtherance of the Business Purpose;
2.1.3 ensure that each such Representative is informed of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information and is bound by confidentiality commitments substantially similar to those set out herein;
2.1.4 not use, exploit, copy, reproduce, reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or derive source code from any Confidential Information, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than the direct evaluation and execution of the Business Purpose; and
2.1.5 refrain from disclosing, publishing, communicating, or transferring any Confidential Information to any third party without the express prior written consent of Vedanta.

2.2 Halyard shall remain responsible and liable for any breach of this Agreement caused by any act, omission, or default of its Representatives to the same extent as if such act or omission had been committed by Halyard itself.

2.3 Halyard shall immediately notify Vedanta in writing upon becoming aware of any actual, suspected, or threatened unauthorised disclosure, loss, compromise, or misappropriation of any Confidential Information, and shall cooperate fully with Vedanta at Halyard's expense to mitigate and remediate any harm arising therefrom.

EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

3.1 The obligations of confidentiality and non-use set forth in Clause 2 shall not apply to any information that Halyard can clearly demonstrate by contemporaneous written evidence:
3.1.1 was already in the public domain at the time of its disclosure to Halyard, or subsequently entered the public domain through no fault, act, or omission of Halyard or its Representatives;
3.1.2 was already lawfully known to Halyard, without any confidentiality obligation or restriction on disclosure, prior to receiving it from Vedanta;
3.1.3 was independently developed by Halyard's personnel without any access to, reliance upon, or reference to the Confidential Information of Vedanta; or
3.1.4 is disclosed by Halyard with the prior written authorisation of an executive officer of Vedanta.

COMPELLED AND LEGAL DISCLOSURES

4.1 If Halyard or any of its Representatives is requested or required by applicable law, court order, or summons issued by a regulatory body or stock exchange of competent jurisdiction to disclose any Confidential Information, Halyard shall:
4.1.1 to the extent permitted by law, provide Vedanta with prompt written notice within three (3) Business Days of receiving such request or demand, so that Vedanta may seek an appropriate protective order, injunctive relief, or other appropriate remedy;
4.1.2 provide reasonable cooperation and assistance to Vedanta, at Vedanta's reasonable expense, in opposing, narrowing, or seeking confidential treatment for such disclosure; and
4.1.3 if disclosure remains legally mandatory, disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is strictly required to satisfy such legal requirement, and use all reasonable endeavours to secure confidential handling of the disclosed information by the relevant authority.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND PROPRIETARY RIGHTS

5.1 Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, as granting, conveying, or conferring upon Halyard any licence, title, ownership, copyright, patent right, trademark, or any other intellectual property right in or to any Confidential Information of Vedanta.

5.2 All Confidential Information, including all copies, extracts, adaptations, and derivative works thereof, shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Vedanta or its licensors.

RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

6.1 Upon the written request of Vedanta, or automatically upon the termination or expiration of this Agreement, Halyard shall promptly, and in any event within fourteen (14) calendar days:
6.1.1 return to Vedanta or, at Vedanta's sole option and discretion, permanently destroy or securely erase all originals, copies, summaries, notes, extracts, models, and electronic files containing or derived from Confidential Information in the possession, custody, or control of Halyard or its Representatives; and
6.1.2 deliver to Vedanta a certificate executed by an authorised officer or director of Halyard confirming full compliance with the provisions of this Clause 6.1.

6.2 Notwithstanding Clause 6.1, Halyard may retain one (1) copy of the Confidential Information solely in its legal archives to the extent strictly necessary to comply with applicable statutory, regulatory, or professional record-retention requirements, provided that any information so retained shall continue to be held in strict confidence in accordance with the terms of this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

Vedanta makes no representations or warranties, whether express, statutory, or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, fitness for a particular purpose, merchantability, or non-infringement of third-party rights with respect to any Confidential Information disclosed hereunder.

EQUITABLE RELIEF

8.1 Halyard acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of the covenants and obligations contained in this Agreement may cause irreparable harm and substantial injury to Vedanta, for which monetary damages alone would not constitute an adequate or complete remedy.

8.2 In the event of any breach or threatened breach by Halyard or its Representatives, Vedanta shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies before any court of competent jurisdiction, without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting any bond or other security, in addition to any other rights and remedies available to it at law or in equity.

TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for an initial term of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier by either Party upon giving thirty (30) calendar days' written notice to the other Party.

9.2 The confidentiality, non-use, and return obligations set out in this Agreement shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement and shall remain binding upon Halyard for a period of three (3) years from the effective date of termination or expiration, except that in respect of any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, such obligations shall survive indefinitely for as long as such information remains a trade secret.

GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement and understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, understandings, arrangements, and negotiations, whether oral or written, between them relating to such subject matter.

10.2 Amendments. No supplement, amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be binding or effective unless made in writing and duly signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

10.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement, or any portion thereof, is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by any court or tribunal of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision shall be construed or modified to the minimum extent necessary to render it valid, legal, and enforceable while giving effect to the commercial intention of the Parties.

10.4 No Assignment. Neither Party may assign, transfer, delegate, sub-contract, or charge any of its rights, benefits, or obligations under this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement upon written notice to an Affiliate or to a successor entity in connection with any merger, consolidation, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

10.5 No Commercial Obligation. Nothing in this Agreement shall obligate either Party to disclose any specific information, to enter into any transaction or commercial relationship, to negotiate any definitive agreement, or to refrain from entering into discussions or transactions with any third party, provided that such discussions or transactions do not violate the terms of this Agreement.

10.6 Notices. Any notice, request, consent, approval, or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given: (a) when delivered personally by hand against written receipt; (b) three (3) Business Days after being dispatched by internationally recognised overnight courier service; or (c) on the date of transmission if sent by email, provided that no bounce-back or automated failure report is received and that a duplicate copy is dispatched by courier within twenty-four (24) hours. Notices shall be addressed to the respective Parties at their registered office addresses set forth at the beginning of this Agreement, marked for the attention of the Legal Department.

10.7 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which when executed and delivered shall be deemed an original, and all of which taken together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic scan, PDF, or electronic signature platform (such as DocuSign or Adobe Sign) shall be as valid, binding, and effective as physical delivery of an ink-signed original.

10.8 Third Party Rights. A person who is not a party to this Agreement shall have no rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 or any other applicable enactment to enforce or enjoy the benefit of any term of this Agreement.

10.9 Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement, and any dispute, controversy, claim, or proceeding arising out of or in connection with it, its subject matter, or its formation (including non-contractual disputes, claims, or tort claims), shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales. The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement.
