BACKGROUND  

(A) The Parties wish to enter into discussions regarding a potential commercial relationship between them (the Purpose).  

(B) In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose certain Confidential Information to the other Party.  The Parties have agreed to regulate the disclosure and use of such Confidential Information on the terms set out in this Agreement.  

AGREED TERMS  

1.  Definitions and Interpretation  

1.1 In this Agreement, the following definitions apply:  

1.1.1 Business Day means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England [[INS: or a federal holiday in the United States]] when banks in London [[INS: and San Francisco]] are open for business.

Confidential Information includes any copies, analyses, compilations, notes or other documents prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent that they contain, reflect or are derived from such information.  

1.1.3 Data Protection Legislation means the UK GDPR (as defined in section 3(10) of the Data Protection Act 2018), the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, and all other applicable laws and regulations relating to the processing of personal data and privacy in force from time to time.  

1.1.4 Disclosing Party means the Party that discloses, or procures the disclosure of, Confidential Information to the other Party under or in connection with this Agreement.  

1.1.5 Group means, in relation to a Party, that Party, any subsidiary or holding company from time to time of that Party, and any subsidiary from time to time of a holding company of that Party.  Each company in a Group is a member of the Group.  

1.1.6 Receiving Party means the Party that receives Confidential Information from or on behalf of the Disclosing Party under or in connection with this Agreement.  

1.1.7 Representatives means, in relation to a Party, the employees, officers, directors, [[INS: independent contractors,]] professional [[DEL: advisers]] [[INS: advisors]], auditors and insurers of that Party and of any member of its Group.  

1.1.8 Trade Secret means any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret within the meaning of Regulation 2 of the Trade Secrets (Enforcement, etc.) Regulations 2018.  

1.2 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  

1.2.1 a reference to a statute or statutory provision is a reference to it as amended, extended or re-enacted from time to time;  

1.2.2 the words "including", "include", "in particular" or any similar expression shall be construed as illustrative and shall not limit the sense of the words preceding those terms;  

1.2.3 a reference to a "person" includes a natural person, corporate or unincorporated body (whether or not having separate legal personality);  

1.2.4 a reference to a "company", "subsidiary" or "holding company" shall be construed in accordance with section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006; and  

1.2.5 a reference to "writing" or "written" includes email.  

2.  Confidentiality Obligations  

2.1 In consideration of the Disclosing Party making Confidential Information available to the Receiving Party, the Receiving Party undertakes to the Disclosing Party that it shall:  

2.1.1 keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information secret and strictly confidential;  

2.1.2 not use or exploit the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in any way except solely for the Purpose;  

2.1.3 not directly or indirectly disclose, or make available, any of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in whole or in part to any third party, except as expressly permitted by this Agreement;  

2.1.4 apply to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information no lesser security measures and degree of care than those which the Receiving Party applies to its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable standard of care, to prevent [[DEL: unauthorised]] [[INS: unauthorized]] disclosure, copying or use; and  

2.1.5 not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, source code or underlying structure of any software, prototypes or other tangible objects provided to it which embody the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.  

2.2 Each Party shall immediately notify the other Party in writing if it becomes aware of any actual or suspected [[DEL: unauthorised]] [[INS: unauthorized]] use, copying or disclosure of any of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and shall provide all reasonable assistance to the Disclosing Party in connection with any action or proceedings which the Disclosing Party may institute in respect of such [[DEL: unauthorised]] [[INS: unauthorized]] use, copying or disclosure.  

3.  Exceptions to Confidentiality  

3.1 The obligations set out in Clause 2 shall not apply to any information which the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written records or other reasonable evidence:  

3.1.1 is in, or enters, the public domain other than as a direct or indirect result of a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives;  

3.1.2 was already lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to its disclosure by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party, free from any restriction as to its use or disclosure;  

3.1.3 is lawfully received by the Receiving Party after the date of this Agreement from an independent third party who is not under any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party in respect of that information; or  

3.1.4 is independently developed by or on behalf of the Receiving Party without access to, use of, or reference to any of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.  

4.  Permitted Disclosures  

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to those of its Representatives and members of its Group who strictly need to know such Confidential Information in order to evaluate or further the Purpose, provided that the Receiving Party shall:  

4.1.1 inform each such Representative and Group member of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information before disclosure is made;  

4.1.2 ensure that each such Representative and Group member is bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality in respect of the Confidential Information which are no less onerous than the terms of this Agreement; and  

4.1.3 remain at all times fully liable to the Disclosing Party for all acts or omissions of its Representatives and members of its Group as if they were the acts or omissions of the Receiving Party itself.  

5.  Compelled Disclosure  

5.1 The Receiving Party may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to the minimum extent required by an order of any court of competent jurisdiction or any regulatory, judicial, governmental or similar body, or by the rules of any recognised stock exchange on which the shares of the Receiving Party or any member of its Group are listed, or by any other applicable law or regulation.  

5.2 Before making any disclosure pursuant to Clause 5.1, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent that it is legally permitted to do so:  

5.2.1 give the Disclosing Party as much prior written notice of the required disclosure as is reasonably practicable in the circumstances;  

5.2.2 consult with the Disclosing Party as to the proposed form, timing, nature and purpose of the disclosure; and  

5.2.3 take all reasonable steps requested by the Disclosing Party (at the Disclosing Party's cost) to resist or narrow the scope of the required disclosure and to obtain confidential treatment or protective orders from the relevant court or authority in respect of the Confidential Information to be disclosed.  

5.3 Where the Receiving Party is unable to inform the Disclosing Party before Confidential Information is disclosed pursuant to Clause 5.1, it shall (to the extent that it is legally permitted to do so) inform the Disclosing Party of the full circumstances of the disclosure and the exact information that has been disclosed as soon as reasonably practicable after such disclosure has been made.  

6.  Return and Destruction of Confidential Information  

6.1 If requested in writing at any time by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall, within ten Business Days of receipt of such request:  

6.1.1 destroy or permanently erase, or (at the Disclosing Party's option) return to the Disclosing Party, all documents and tangible materials in its possession, custody or control that contain or record any of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;  

6.1.2 procure that its Representatives and members of its Group to whom Confidential Information has been disclosed take the steps referred to in Clause 6.1.1; and  

6.1.3 confirm in writing to the Disclosing Party, by a statement signed by a duly [[DEL: authorised]] [[INS: authorized]] officer of the Receiving Party, that it has complied in full with its obligations under this Clause 6.1.  

6.2 Notwithstanding Clause 6.1, the Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain:  

6.2.1 copies of any Confidential Information that are securely stored in routine, automated electronic backup or archiving systems which are not readily accessible in the ordinary course of business; and  

6.2.2 such copies of Confidential Information as are strictly required to be retained by applicable law, regulation, professional standards or the rules of any competent regulatory authority to which the Receiving Party or the relevant Representative is subject.  

6.3 Any Confidential Information retained by the Receiving Party or its Representatives pursuant to Clause 6.2 shall remain subject to the confidentiality and non-use obligations set out in this Agreement for so long as such information is retained, notwithstanding the expiry or termination of this Agreement.  

7.  Ownership, No Licence and No Warranty  

7.1 All Confidential Information, and all intellectual property rights in or relating to it, shall remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party (or its licensors, as applicable).  

7.2 No right or [[DEL: licence]] [[INS: license]], express or implied, is granted to the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in respect of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights of the Disclosing Party, other than the limited right to use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  

7.3 All Confidential Information is provided by the Disclosing Party on an "as is" basis.  Neither Party makes any representation or gives any warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, adequacy or fitness for a particular purpose of any Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement.  

7.4 Neither the Disclosing Party nor any of its Representatives shall have any liability to the Receiving Party or to any other person resulting from the use of, or reliance upon, the Confidential Information by the Receiving Party, save that nothing in this Agreement shall exclude or limit either Party's liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.  

7.5 Nothing in this Agreement shall oblige either Party to disclose any particular information, to enter into any further agreement or transaction with the other Party, or to proceed with the Purpose or any commercial relationship.  

8.  Publicity  

8.1 Neither Party shall make, or permit any person to make, any public announcement, press release or other public disclosure concerning the existence or subject matter of this Agreement, the Purpose, or the fact that discussions are taking place between the Parties without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law or any governmental or regulatory authority in accordance with Clause 5.  

9.  Data Protection  

9.1 The Parties do not anticipate that the disclosure of Confidential Information for the Purpose will involve the sharing or processing of any personal data (as defined in the Data Protection Legislation), other than routine business contact details of the Parties' respective personnel involved in managing the discussions.  

9.2 If either Party identifies that it is necessary to disclose any other personal data to the other Party in connection with the Purpose, that Party shall not disclose such personal data unless and until the Parties have agreed and entered into a separate written data processing agreement or data sharing agreement that complies with the requirements of the Data Protection Legislation.  

10.  Term and Survival  

10.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the date set out at the head of this Agreement and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier by either Party giving not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.  

10.2 Expiry or termination of this Agreement shall not affect any accrued rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the Parties existing at the date of expiry or termination.  

10.3 The obligations of confidentiality and non-use contained in this Agreement shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years from the effective date of expiry or termination, save that in respect of any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, such obligations shall continue in full force and effect for so long as that information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law.
